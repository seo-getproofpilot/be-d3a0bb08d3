11FAQ

Questions people ask before they call.

How much does solar detach and reset cost?

It depends on the panel count, the roof type (tile, shingle or flat), the racking and whether any panels or parts need replacing. We quote it after seeing the system, and roofers can get a quote to drop straight into their proposal.

Will you work directly with my roofing company?

Yes. Roofing companies around the Valley send us their solar jobs. We schedule the detach around the tear-off and come back for the reset once the new roof is on.

My solar installer went out of business. Can you service my system?

Yes. We diagnose and repair systems from any installer, work warranty claims with the manufacturer for you, and offer extended warranty options for systems whose installer is gone.

Do I need a panel upgrade to add an EV charger?

Not always. It depends on your panel's capacity and what's already on it. We do a load check first, and if the panel can carry a 240V charger circuit, you won't need the upgrade.

Can you add a 240V circuit for a sauna, spa or shop?

Yes. We check what your panel can carry, size the wire and breaker to the equipment, run the circuit and get it inspected. If the panel is full, we'll tell you what it takes before any work starts.

Are you licensed in Arizona?

Yes. Black Out Electric AZ LLC holds Arizona Registrar of Contractors licenses #362987 and #362988.

Do you offer financing?

Yes, through Service Finance, Lyon Financial and Foundation Finance. Approval is up to the lender, and having three lenders gives you more than one shot at approval.

What areas and hours do you work?

We're based in Mesa and work across the Phoenix Valley, with larger projects statewide and in Las Vegas. The shop is open Monday to Saturday, 6 AM to 6 PM. Call (480) 939-0234.
